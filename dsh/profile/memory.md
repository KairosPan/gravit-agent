Memory. `mcp__memory__*` is the owner's record of decisions and lessons; its own instructions say how. What this host adds:
- Spell a symbol as the Market does: a US ticker in capitals (`NVDA`), an A share as its six digits (`600519`). `market` is `US` or `CN`.
- Register your own call (`whose: agent`) before you place an order. Register what the owner decides here or by phone (`whose: owner`), in their words. When the owner says a position is closed, amend its decision with `close`.
- Only the conversation, in the owner's turn, writes `whose: owner` or amends the owner's decision.
- The face registers the owner's answer to an order card itself: never register it.
- Name every fact's source. For a lesson card prefer the facts the face computes: `price`, `change_1d_pct`, `change_5d_pct`, `change_20d_pct`, `change_60d_pct`, `held`, `unrealized_pct`.
- `mcp__account__portfolio_memory` and `portfolio_remember` are the seat's notes at the Account: no decision and no lesson goes there.
