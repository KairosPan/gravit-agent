## 1. Intent

One front end. The operator's words from the start were that Linqi's Markets and Account were to be grafted into Gravit, not run beside it; what landed on 2026-10-21 was two consoles — the face at 127.0.0.1:3090 drawing the alpaca-kit paper account, and Linqi's page at 127.0.0.1:4820 where the Account lives and the owner signs — joined only by Gravit's seat. The word "B" meant three things in three documents (the handoff's *A · Gravit as the Account's tenant* is what was built; the operator's *B* was "接好账户和交易所 connector", the Account's and its venues' content in the face's screens).

This design makes the face's **Markets** and **Account** screens show the Account's content — its markets across its venues and the public listings, its net worth, holdings, positions, orders, statement, venues, agents and the cards waiting — and makes the face's page an **owner device** of the Account, so the operator trades by hand there and signs what the Account's door asks for, without opening the Account's page. The seat is untouched: it stays the only way Gravit acts on the Account, under both cards. The Account's page remains for what the face does not draw (§2 non-goals): the face links to it from one place.

The hard fact that shapes the mechanism: the Account answers only its own pages. A page at another origin — the face's, or an `<iframe>` of the Account's page inside the face — is refused by its fence (`src/portfolio/server.ts` ~450–470: a foreign `Origin`, or a `Sec-Fetch-Site` of `same-site`/`cross-site` that is not a top-level navigation, is 403). A plain loopback `fetch` from the face process, with no `Origin`, passes — `face/src/seat.ts` already reads `GET /api/account` that way. So the face **proxies** the Account's HTTP and SSE under its own `/data/account/*`, behind its own `/data` fence, and the face's page talks to the Account through the face. The face process carries bytes; it holds no key and signs nothing. The owner's device key is the browser's, as it is on the Account's page.

The round's purpose, in the operator's words (2026-10-22): **借鉴 Account 完善 Gravit** — improve Gravit by learning from the Account: its keyless, server-side market data subscribed by what the page is looking at; its venues as accounts the owner connects with a key; its one console; its door protocol. This design is the first step on that road, and it clears the ground: the previous product's own market and account layer — `alpaca_kit`, the PIT beds, backtests — leaves with it (§12, the operator's word), so that Gravit's markets and account are the Account's. What is left for the next steps is in §2.

Why not the alternatives: vendoring the Account's page (`src/portfolio/public/ui`, ~31,000 lines of its own shell, tokens and streams) is a second product inside the first and a moving target (26 pull requests in twelve days); mounting Gravit inside the Account's page (the front-end design's §9.1) makes the Account the console, which is the opposite of the operator's intent; and gravit-ui's Markets, market page and Trade were drawn after the Account's own pages (§15), so re-feeding them from the Account's routes is the short path.

## 2. Goals · non-goals · anti-goals

**Goals**
1. **Markets is the Account's Markets, and its market data comes the Account's way.** The table lists what the Account can trade and watch — its connected venues' markets and the public listings it reads without keys, server-side: four venues' public WebSockets for crypto (Hyperliquid, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX), Robinhood and Nasdaq polled for US stocks with Cboe's delayed quotes behind them, Kalshi, Polymarket and Polymarket US for predictions — by the Account's kinds (All · Crypto · Stocks · RWAs · Perps · Pre-IPO · Predictions, shown only when non-empty, as the Account shows them) with its sorts, every price carrying the Account's own source and age (`quoteFrom`, `quoteAt`, `delayMs`). The page says what it is looking at and the Account streams or polls accordingly, as its own page does. The face's own keyed providers (Alpaca's IEX stream, Coinbase) leave the screens; only A shares, which the Account has no source for, stay on the face's own wire, labelled (§6.1).
2. **A market's page** draws from the Account: price and 24h, the facts, Across venues (the row's legs, the Account's `compare` for the fit), the chart from the Account's `candles` (its ranges 1D · 5D · 1M · 3M · 6M · YTD · 1Y · 5Y · MAX), You hold and the orders on it, Gravit on it, On the statement; Buy and Sell lead to the Trade ticket. A face-only id keeps today's page (the face's bars, no venues).
3. **Account is the Account.** Portfolio = net worth with its change line and the curve (`history`), Assets across venues, Positions, Open orders; Statement = the Account's statement rows with the CSV built in the browser; Venues = the Account's venues with health, `Paper` chips, Details, and the owner's keys (Connect an account for key-file venues, Open a paper account, Disconnect); Agents = every seat the Account knows with its standing and limits — Gravit's row first, from the seat view the face already computes — with Let in and Give a limit; Cards = **Waiting for you**, the Account's Guard cards, each with What it asks and ✓ Approve / ✗ Reject. The rail's net worth under Account is the Account's.
4. **Trade returns to the rail**: the owner's hand order through the Account's own ticket — the market, Where (the venues that trade it, best price marked), Buy or Sell, the amount in dollars or units, Market or Limit (cents for a prediction), the quote the Account prepares, **What you sign** (every field of the prepared action, verbatim), its validity, Sign and place; the fill reaches Portfolio and the Statement at once through the Account's stream. A hand order is the owner's act: it passes no card of the face's and no card of the Account's, only the Account's own live cap and the venue's rules.
5. **The face's page is an owner device.** It makes a non-extractable device key in the browser, pairs (the pairing code on a fresh Account; otherwise an owner device lets it in under Devices), and signs every owner action in the Account's envelope exactly as the Account's page does. The face process proxies the bytes, logs nothing of them, holds no key and signs nothing (charter §7.3, D18).
6. **Honest states.** An unreachable Account, a page that is not yet an owner device, a stale read, an Account that moved its API: each is said in one muted line where it applies, and no screen shows a number it did not read from the Account. Nothing scripted, nothing stand-in.
7. **Gravit's path is unchanged**: the seat row, its 35 tools, Gate 2's cards for the seat's writes, the receipts. The Account's Guard card for a seat write is now answered in the face's Cards (goal 3) — two cards per write, both in the face.
8. **The documents say what is built**: the charter (the MARKET and ACCOUNT lines, the write map, §7.3, a new debt D18, §8, §9), `DEVELOPMENT.md`, `face/README.md` (the two screens, the proxy, the device, the drill), `CLAUDE.md`, `GRAVIT.md`, the ledger; the seat design is marked superseded where this one changes it.
9. **The three components leave** (§12): `alpaca_kit`, the PIT beds and backtests go, replaced by the Account through the seat and AKShare; what needs the operator's word is listed in §12.3.

**Non-goals (this round, by the operator's order and by scope)**
- **Guard · Beast and the live cap** ("先不管guard/beast"): the head's chip reads the Account's mode and cap; changing either stays on the Account's page (Settings). Nothing here opens Beast.
- Memory, Desk, Brains, Superagent, Earn, Money (Move · Receive · Hand to agent · Sell many · bridge routes), agent wallets, Lens, the ASCII ocean — the Account's page keeps them; the face draws none of them and no key leads there but the one link (§6.5).
- Venues that connect through a sign-in page or a browser wallet (Robinhood's OAuth callback, OKX Wallet / MetaMask proofs) — bound to the Account's origin; they connect on the Account's page and then appear in the face's Venues like any other.
- Perpetuals, pre-IPO perpetuals, leverage, stop orders, time in force, amend: listed in Markets as the Account lists them, not tradeable from the face's ticket this round (the front-end design's §15.5 kept them out; *call*). Cancel of a resting order is in.
- Real venues for the **seat**: the paper-only rule for Gravit's limits stands (seat design §2, charter D17). The owner's hand orders are the owner's: the ticket says `Paper` or the venue's name, and the Account's cap bounds each.
- Retiring the face's own producers (`/data/account.json`, `/data/market.json`, `/data/watchlist.json`, `scripts/face_data.py`): the Account screen stops reading `/data/account.json`; the routes stay for a round, marked compatibility-only (*call*), deletion a listed follow-up. A consequence, stated so it is chosen and not stumbled on (*call*): the face's own alpaca-kit paper account — the one Gravit's `place_order` would reach behind Gate 1 and Gate 2, never armed in the real home — is on no screen any more. To see it, the operator connects the same Alpaca paper key file to the Account (`live:alpaca-paper`, from the face's Connect an account in stage 2), and it is then one of the Account's venues like any other; a receipt's reload reads the Account, which knows that venue's fills. This round changes nothing in `alpaca_kit`; its keyed half is the wallet of the bullet below.
- A second market-data system beside the Account's: no keyless Robinhood/Nasdaq reader of the face's own, no WebSocket client of its own — the Account already does this, and the face reads through it (the operator's "数据源参考linqi的做法"). The face's Alpaca and Coinbase quote providers, `/data/bars` and the US/crypto half of the symbol search are unread by any screen after stage 1 and stay registered this round, deletion a follow-up with the producers above (*call*).
- **A keyed source is a wallet, and later an MCP server** (the operator's "带钥匙的可以理解为钱包，后续做成MCP"): in the Account's vocabulary a source that needs a key is not a quote feed but an account the owner connects — Alpaca with its keys is such an account, connected to the Account as a venue when the operator wants it in view (§6.2), and in a later round offered to Gravit as an MCP server of its own, the way the Account's seat is one. Nothing of that MCP round is built here; this design reads the Account and holds no key.
- One card per seat write (a request to Linqi, unchanged).

**Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)**
- The face quoting a market the Account covers from a source of its own: one price per row, the Account's, with its `quoteFrom` and age; the face's wire speaks only where the Account is silent (A shares).
- A door around Gate 2 for Gravit: the seat's writes stay in the gate's list. The proxied owner routes (`pair`, `prepare`, `exchange`) open no door the Account's own loopback surface does not already have — a local process reaches `POST /api/exchange` at 4820 today as it will reach `/data/account/exchange`, and either does nothing without an envelope signed by a device key the Account admitted (the seat's R1 already names the shell turn that signs with the seat's key file; D18 names the device). The proxy must not widen this: no cookie is minted, no key is held, no envelope is made server-side.
- A key or a signer in the face **process**: no key file, no signing code outside the browser; the proxy never reads, logs or echoes a body.
- The face modelling the Account's rules: what an order is worth, whether it fits the cap, what a movement costs — the Account's `prepare` answers; the face draws the answer and never computes its own.
- A screen that shows the Account's numbers as the face's own, or the face's quotes as the Account's: every row names its source where the two meet (§6.1).
- Vendoring the Account's page or modules, pinning a commit, a submodule.

## 3. Constraints

- The charter as amended by §8: one operator, loopback only, the two gates on Gravit's tool surface, no authentication machinery of the face's own (the tokenized cookie is dsh's), the owner's device key the browser's.
- The product speaks English; screens carry no explanatory small print (front-end decision 9) — the Account's own refusal sentences are shown as its words, in its words; host status lines are the one muted line.
- Vanilla ES modules, no bundler, no framework; `tsx --test`; the frame fixture unchanged (the thread is untouched).
- The face's `/data` fence (`isTrustedDataRequest`: loopback host, no cross-site, same-origin `Origin`) on every proxied route; the Account's fence passed by a bare loopback fetch; `FACE_ACCOUNT_URL` names the Account (default `http://127.0.0.1:4820`), as the seat does.
- The Account's API is pinned nowhere: the maps are dated at 133b9a7, the smoke against the stand-in is the canary, a changed shape turns a block into "unreadable", never a wrong number (the seat's rule, kept).
- WebCrypto in the browser: `127.0.0.1` is a secure context, so `crypto.subtle` and a non-extractable ECDSA P-256 key exist on the face's page as on the Account's.
- The Account's own limits on a consumer: `account`, `holdings`, `statement` and `agents` settle venue polls for seconds; `candles` and `history` ask a source per page; sixteen followed markets per stream and a hundred across its hub, thirty-two streams in all; the region check every thirty minutes is the Account's.

## 5. The face server

### 5.1 The proxy (`face/src/account-proxy.ts`)

One registrar, `registerAccountProxy(webServer, { url, fetch, now })`, registering `exact` routes under `/data/account/…` — one per allowed Account route, never a prefix catch-all, so the allow-list is the route table and nothing else of the Account's surface is reachable through the face:

The TTLs mirror the Account's own page (`docs/research/2026-10-22-account-read-api-map.md`, the appendix), so the face never asks the Account more often than its page would — the reads built on `accountView()` (`account`, `holdings`, `statement`, `agents`, `history?paper=1`) poll the venues each time they run.

| Face route | Account route | Method | Stage | Query names allowed | Cache · abort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/data/account` | `GET /api/account` | GET | 1 | `fresh` | 15 s · 7 s; one shared reader, which `seat.ts` now reads through (*call*) |
| `/data/account/explore` | `GET /api/account/explore` | GET | 1 | `tab`, `q` (≤ 60), `key`, `limit` (1–200) | 20 s · 10 s |
| `/data/account/market`, `quotes` | the same | GET | 1 | `venue`, `symbol` · `pairs` (≤ 12) | 3 s · 7 s |
| `/data/account/compare` | `GET /api/account/compare` | GET | 1 | `base`, `side`, `usd`, `asset` | 15 s · 7 s |
| `/data/account/candles` | `GET /api/account/candles` | GET | 1 | `venue`, `symbol`, `interval`, `range`, `before`, `limit` | 30 s · 25 s (a first view waits for a source) |
| `/data/account/holdings` | `GET /api/account/holdings` | GET | 1 | `cost`, `paper`, `fresh` | 8 s · 12 s |
| `/data/account/positions` | `GET /api/account/positions` | GET | 1 | `venue` | 10 s · 7 s |
| `/data/account/statement` | `GET /api/account/statement` | GET | 1 | `fresh` | 10 s · 7 s |
| `/data/account/history` | `GET /api/account/history` | GET | 1 | `range`, `paper`, `venue` | 60 s · 12 s |
| `/data/account/agents` | `GET /api/account/agents` | GET | 1 | `fresh` | 15 s · 7 s |
| `/data/account/stream` | `GET /api/account/stream` | GET (SSE) | 1 | `since`, `client` (8–48 of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`), `markets` | one upstream per browser connection, closed with it |
| `/data/account/stream/markets` | `POST /api/account/stream/markets` | POST | 1 | — | 4 KB body |
| `/data/account/venues` | `GET /api/account/venues` | GET | 2 | — (never `force`) | 60 s · 30 s (a cold catalog probes every venue) |
| `/data/account/connect/reach`, `keyfile`, `paper/books` | the same | GET | 2 | `connector` · `kind`, `venue`, `ref`, `exchange` · — | 5 s · 30 s |
| `/data/account/pair` | `POST /api/account/pair` | POST | 2 | — | 4 KB body |
| `/data/account/prepare` | `POST /api/account/prepare` | POST | 2 | — | 16 KB body |
| `/data/account/exchange` | `POST /api/exchange` | POST | 2 | — | 64 KB body |

Rules, all of them the seat route's, generalised:
- The face's fence first (403, the fixed body), then 405 for a method the row does not name, then the request. A query is rebuilt from the whitelisted names with bounded values (≤ 240 characters each, each name once); an unknown name, a repeated one or an over-long value is 400 `invalid account request`. The raw query string never reaches the Account.
- The same plain loopback `fetch` to `FACE_ACCOUNT_URL` that `seat.ts` uses (no `Origin`, no `Sec-Fetch-Site`, no cookie, the URL's own `Host`; undici's `sec-fetch-mode` is not read by the Account's fence, and the seat smoke proves the fence passes), `redirect: "error"`, the abort above on the headers and the body, the Account's status and JSON passed through as they are (its `{ok:false, refusal:{…}}` is the page's to draw, in the Account's words; `ok` is not uniform across its reads, and the client reads each as the map says).
- A failure of the fetch itself is a state, not an error: `503 {"ok":false,"unreachable":"<CODE>"}` with the failure's code (`ECONNREFUSED`, `timeout`, `unreadable`), never its text; nothing served stale for a write; a cached read is re-served flagged `stale:true` as `data.ts` does.
- GET caches per route with the TTLs above, single-flight, at most 64 keys per route, keyed by the rebuilt query; `?fresh=1` skips the TTL and never joins a plain read in flight (the seat route's rule). The Account's own `cache-control: no-store` is kept on the face's answer.
- POST bodies are read under the stated limit (`readBody`), passed as bytes with `content-type: application/json`, never parsed, never logged, never echoed — the pairing code and the signed envelope travel through the face once and leave no trace in it; a body over the limit is 413.
- The SSE route: per browser connection the face opens one upstream request with `accept: text/event-stream`, passing `since` (or the browser's `Last-Event-ID` as `since`), `client` and `markets` as the browser sent them; the frames are piped through unchanged (ids, `retry:`, the Account's `hb` heartbeat); the upstream is closed when the browser closes, and a browser connection is refused `503` with the Account's sentence when the Account refuses (its 32-stream cap). The browser mints its own `client` id (`face-<24 hex>`, one per tab, the Account's grammar) and sends the same id in `stream/markets`, which is forwarded as posted (the Account keys the follow set by `client`); the proxy rewrites neither.
- `fresh` is the face's own flag and is never forwarded. The Account's URL is the one the seat row is composed with (`composedSeatRow`, as `seat.ts` reads it — an operator's patch counts), falling back to `FACE_ACCOUNT_URL`; a disabled seat row disables the proxy too, and every route then answers `503 {"ok":false,"unreachable":"disabled"}`.
- Never proxied: `overview` (the classic page's read), `read` (a ledger row per call), `markets`, `quote`, `brain-key`, `brain-test`, `signin/*`, `wallet/*`, `mode`, `revoke`, `restore`, `reset`, `say`, `approve`, `execute`, `order`, `desk/*`, `memory/*`, `earn`, `bridge-routes`, `receive`, `sellable`, `funding`, `asset`, `book`, `trades`, `live/*`, `exchanges` — the Account's page's, or doors this design does not open. A route not in the table is the face's ordinary 404; nothing is forwarded by prefix.

### 5.2 What does not change
`account-seat.ts`, `orders.ts`, the policy layer, the rows, Gate 2, the receipts. `seat.ts` keeps `/data/seat.json` and `seatView`; only its fetch moves behind the proxy's shared `/api/account` reader. `quotes.ts` keeps `/data/quotes` and its stream, subscribed after stage 1 for `cn` ids only (the iFinD provider; the Alpaca and Coinbase providers stay registered and idle); `bars.ts`, the us/crypto half of `symbol-search.ts` and `data.ts`'s three producer routes stay registered and unread by any screen (§2).

## 6. The client

### 6.1 Markets (stage 1)
- **Rows** are the Account's `explore` items (the row's id is the item's `key` — `coin:BTC`, `stock:AAPL`, `perp:ETH`, `rwa:NVDA`, `kalshi:…`, `pm:…`, `pmus:…`; its `kind` and `tabs` pick the tab; its `at[]` legs, `price`, `changePct24h`, `volumeUsd24h`, `quoteFrom`, `quoteAt` are the row's), plus the face's `cn` rows from its own watchlist and search, quoted by the face's wire (the iFinD provider), under Stocks. A row carries `source: 'account' | 'face'` and the source word is drawn beside its quote's state — the Account's rows in the Account's own words ("Robinhood · real time", "Cboe · 15 min delayed", "Coinbase · live") — so the two never look alike by accident. No `us:` or `crypto:` row is quoted by the face any more (goal 1): the operator's existing `us:`/`crypto:` watchlist entries are asked of the Account once (`explore?q=`) and become its rows, and one the Account cannot find is listed once under a muted "Not listed at the Account" line with Remove (*call*). Tabs: the Account's `tabs` as it sends them (All · Crypto · Stocks · RWAs · Perps · Pre-IPO · Predictions, only when non-empty), Watching once something is starred, All results while searching.
- **Prices by kind**, as the Account's page draws them: an event's `price` is dollars per contract and its 24h `change24h` dollars per contract (drawn in cents), Kalshi volume is `contracts24h`; a pre-IPO perpetual belongs to Pre-IPO (its `implied`), with the contract's `price`; a stock carries `quoteFrom` · `quoteAt` · `delayMs` and the age is drawn beside a price older than five seconds; a paper venue's price is its source's listing.
- **Icons**: the Account's rows name third-party image URLs (`icon`: Elbstream, Kalshi, Polymarket, Google favicons). The face's page does not load them — the tile is the face's own letters, as today — so a row never makes the browser reach a third party (*call*, the charter's loopback posture; the Google Fonts link stays the one exception already recorded).
- **Search**: the Account's `explore?q=<text>&limit=200` (its own search across its venues' symbol books and the public sources) plus the face's `/data/symbols/search` for `cn` only — the hits under one head with their source words.
- **Sorts**: Most traded · Biggest moves · Closing soonest (the last only with events), on the table's rows in the browser (the Account's page sorts client-side too).
- **The tradeable universe** is what the Account's `explore` lists and finds: with `q` it asks every connected venue's own `markets(q)` and every public source's search (the US stocks feed answers from its whole symbol book, so a paper account trades any US symbol the feed knows), not only the forty busiest it lists cold. So a `us:` or `crypto:` id from the face's old watchlist, or typed into the search, is asked of the Account (`explore?q=<symbol>&limit=12`); found, it is the Account's row and page (with its venues and Buy/Sell); not found, the "Not listed at the Account" line — the face quotes no such market of its own (goal 1). `cn` ids never ask (*call*; the Account has no A shares). `/api/account/markets` stays unproxied.
- **A market's page**: for an Account id — price and 24h, the facts (`market` for a connected venue's symbol, else the row's leg), **Across venues** from the row's `at[]` legs (connected ones with their prices, the best marked; public legs with Connect to trade leading to Venues; `compare` refines the best and the fit once the ticket names an amount), the chart from `candles` with the Account's ranges 1D · 5D · 1M · 3M · 6M · YTD · 1Y · 5Y · MAX (`render/chart.js` gains a range seg and the Account's `ends`/`note` line), You hold (`holdings?cost=1` rows and `positions` for the market) with its open orders (from `account.orders`) and Cancel (stage 2), Gravit on it (the sessions whose title names the market, as today), On the statement (`statement` lines for the market); keys Buy · Sell (where held) · Hand to Gravit · Watch — Buy and Sell open the Trade ticket on this market (stage 2; until then the two keys are absent, not disabled). For a `cn` id — today's page unchanged (the face's quote, no chart, no venues).
- **Refresh follows the Account's own model** — the page says what it is looking at: Markets re-reads `explore` every 20 s while on screen in a visible tab and asks `quotes?pairs=` (twelve a call, every 5 s) only for the rows in view, a prediction card only while visible; a market's page follows its market on the Account's stream (`stream/markets`, `<venue>:<symbol>`, `~` for price alone), which the Account streams for Hyperliquid, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX and the paper markets priced from them, and polls `quotes` for the rest; the one stream per tab carries the account events too (§6.2); a hidden tab closes its stream and lets its follows go, and reopens with `Last-Event-ID` on return. The face's own wire (`/data/quotes/stream`) is subscribed for `cn` rows only.

### 6.3 The owner's device (stage 2, `host/owner.js`)
The Account's own `owner.js` (148 lines) reproduced for the face's origin, with its storage and `crypto.subtle` injected so the same module runs under Node 22 in the tests. Byte for byte per `docs/research/2026-10-22-account-owner-path-map.md` §1–§2; the names below are the Account's.
- **The key**: `crypto.subtle.generateKey({ name: "ECDSA", namedCurve: "P-256" }, false, ["sign", "verify"])` — non-extractable; the `CryptoKeyPair` kept in IndexedDB (database `gravit-face-owner`, store `keys`, record `device`; *call*: the face's own name, the origin being the face's); made on first need, deleted on sign-out; `kid` = the first 16 hex of `sha256(x ‖ y)` of the public JWK, computed locally and checked against the pair answer.
- **Standing**, read off every `/data/account` read: this page's `device:<kid>` among `signers.owners` → *signs*; among `signers.pendingDevices` → *waiting to be let in*; in neither and the Account has no owner (`signers.owners` empty) → *pair with the code*; in neither and the Account has owners → *ask to sign* (one key: Pair); the sign-out flag set → *signed out*; no WebCrypto → *cannot sign here*. The device line in the Account head says which, in one muted sentence, and the Devices box in Settings draws the owners (`label`), the pending devices (`kid`, since) each with **Let it sign**, and this page's **Sign out**.
- **Pairing**: `POST /data/account/pair { jwk: {kty, crv, x, y}, label: "Gravit face", code? }` → `{ kid, role: "owner" | "pending" | "needs-code" }`. On an ownerless Account the code typed into the box makes this page the owner (five wrong tries end it until the Account restarts — the Account's rule, drawn in its words). Otherwise the page is pending: the Account's own page lists it under Settings › Devices ("A browser asked to sign") and an owner device presses **Let it sign** there once; the face re-reads and the line turns to *signs*. **Let it sign** in the face is the same owner action from this device: `convertToMultiSigUser { signers: JSON of { authorizedUsers: [...owners, "device:<kid>"] sorted without repeats, threshold: 1 } }` — so once the face's page signs, a second face browser or the Account's own page is admitted from the face.
- **The envelope**, exactly the Account's: `message = { accountChain: prepared.accountChain, ...every prepared.shown[].name → value }` (every value a string); `input = canonical({ domain: prepared.domain, primaryType: prepared.primaryType, message })` where `canonical` is `JSON.stringify` over keys sorted recursively, `undefined` dropped, arrays in order, no whitespace; `es256 = base64url(ECDSA P-256 / SHA-256 over the UTF-8 bytes)`, raw `r ‖ s`, no padding; the body `{ action: prepared.action, nonce: prepared.action.nonce, signature: { kid, es256 } }` to `POST /data/account/exchange`. The nonce is prepare's, on the Account's clock — never the browser's.
- **Every owner action is prepare → What you sign → sign**: `POST /data/account/prepare { draft }` → `Prepared { action, primaryType, domain, accountChain, shown[], quote? }`; the sheet draws the quote's `words` and figures above a `What you sign` block that lists `shown[]` verbatim as `name: value` lines (the Account's trust core, kept whole); a money action's `deadline` is drawn as `Your signature is good for m:ss more`, re-prepared at zero and when under thirty seconds on submit; a form changed since the preparation re-prepares instead of signing. A refusal at prepare is drawn in the Account's `refusal.message` and nothing is signed.
- **The answer**: `200` with `kind` `account` · `order` · `payment` · `result`, or `401`/`409` with `refusal`; a `kind: "result"` whose `result.ok === false` is a refusal too (a rejected card, the Account's own convention). A replay within two days returns the first answer, so a retry is never a second execution. `E_ACCOUNT_REQUOTE` and `E_ACCOUNT_EXPIRED` offer one key, *Prepare again*; `E_ACCOUNT_BAD_SIGNATURE` on a page that thinks it signs re-reads the standing (the Account restarted, or the device was dropped) and says so.
- **Sign out**: `signOut { device: "device:<kid>" }` signed by this page; on success the flag is set and the key deleted; the last device out makes the Account print a new pairing code in its own terminal (the face cannot read it; the line says where it is).
- **Never**: export the key, hold a key in the face process, sign outside the browser, take a nonce from the face's clock, log or keep the code or an envelope, raise `threshold`. An EOA owner held by the face process (the map's inferred option) is rejected by §2's anti-goal.

### 6.4 Trade and the other owner keys (stage 2)

- **Agents**: **Let in…** = `approveAgent { agentAddress, agentName, validUntil }` for a knocking key from `account.requests` (1 · 7 · 30 · 90 days; the Account caps at 180); **Give a limit…** = `approveSpend { agent, scope: "trade", allow: "<venue ids, comma-separated>", perPayment, budget, windowHours: 0, validUntil }` — `allow` is a pick list of the Account's venues, paper first, and `*` is not offered (the seat's rule and the README's); **End limit** = the same with `perPayment: "0"`, `budget: "0"`; **Revoke…** = `approveAgent` with the zero address and the key's name, `validUntil: 0` (final for that address, the Account's rule, said before signing). - **Cards**: ✓ Approve / ✗ Reject = `approveCard { card: id, action: card.hash, decision }` — one card, one signature; no approve-all (*call*). Approve re-runs the agent's instruction at the Account (its key, limit and venue judged again); the answer is the order or the Account's refusal, and the card leaves on the stream's word. - **Watch** on an Account row (§6.1): `setWatch { venue, symbol, on: "true" | "" }`.
- **Settings › Devices** (§6.3): Let it sign, Sign out.
- Not drawn, by §2: `setPolicy` (the cap, Beast, the session, leverage), `liveMove`, `liveEarn`, `liveClose`, `liveLeverage`, `liveAmend`, `setIntent` (Hand to Gravit is the face's own composer), Memory, the desk, brains, agent wallets.
